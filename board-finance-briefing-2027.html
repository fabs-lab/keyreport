08 | Board decisions

Adopt a cost-reduction program, not another passive forecast.

Decide now

  • Use the adjusted trend case as the 2027 planning baseline.
  • Set a 50% front-desk cost target subject to service-level approval.
  • Set the maximum acceptable all-in gap and reserve funding level.
  • Require a monthly cost and reserve transfer dashboard.

Deliver within 90 days

  • Rebid or narrow maintenance contracts.
  • Complete the asset and preventive-maintenance inventory.
  • Adopt an owner-responsibility and carpet-chargeback policy.
  • Obtain solar feasibility and utility-use proposals.
  • Document in-house work boundaries and approvals.
Recommended planning approach: assign owners, set measurable reduction targets, strengthen maintenance controls, and review progress monthly.
