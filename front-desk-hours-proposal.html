Front desk labor is two different cost lines. ABC bills concierge hours. Jennifer Marshall is Assistant GM on salary (passthrough) - not an ABC hourly rate. Cutting desk hours saves ABC dollars; it does not by itself change Jennifer’s salary.

Jennifer is not ABC.

Current base $52,000 (2026). Carlton all-in passthrough at that base ≈ $66,701 (salary + health + payroll taxes + workers’ comp + 401k). Board path for 2027 is about $55,000 base (≈ $70k all-in estimated). That line already covers paying her during PTO; it does not cover ABC desk replacement while she is out. It continues whether the lobby is staffed evenings or not, unless her role changes separately.

What the ~$70k does and does not cover

In ~$70,066: Jennifer’s employment cost for the year - salary, labor burden / benefits path, FSR HR fee. When she takes PTO, that pay continues inside this line (no second Jennifer paycheck). Not in ~$70,066: someone else at the desk while she is out. That is ABC (or other) hourly, stacked on top in Scenario 2. She has 120 PTO hours this year (board/FSR retention). At $25.95, desk backfill = $3,114/year (already in Scenario 2). If PTO rises (e.g. 160 hrs), add ~$1,038 more. Board/FSR terms are 120 hours total PTO with no separate sick bank. Scenario 3 has no Jennifer leave backfill line.

Scenario 2 · Keep Jennifer

Pay Jennifer ~$70k all-in (PTO pay included). Staff evenings/weekends with ABC (~58 hrs), plus ABC daytime when she is on PTO (not inside the $70k). Save 37.6% vs today with 120 PTO hrs. Keeps AGM capacity in the building.

Scenario 3 · ABC only

Drop the ~$70k Jennifer line and her PTO desk-backfill invoice. All 98 open hours at ABC rates. Cheapest cash cost (−45.5% vs today) only if her AGM role is not retained. If she stays as AGM off-desk, add ~$70k back (~$202k total) - still without a Jennifer desk-backfill line if she is not on desk.

Rate note for the board

Use $25.95/hr (Carlton’s 2027 ABC table) as the budget baseline. An unsigned “ABC Proposed time Change 2026” workbook used $29.95 and a different 84-hour schedule (save ~$68.5k in that sheet) - treat that as a vendor draft, not the signed rate. Confirm final rate and hours in writing with ABC before budgeting the savings.

Tradeoffs

What changes for residents

Covered well

Afternoon and early-evening package pickup on weekdays, and daytime weekend claims. About 4 in 5 package releases fall inside the proposed desk hours.

Falls outside

About 19% of claims (889 of 4,739) happen outside the proposed windows - mainly weeknights after 10pm, plus Sunday evenings after 7pm and early Saturday mornings before 9am. Those pickups would wait until the next open shift unless another option is offered.

Board decision

Adopt Mon–Fri 7am–10pm, Saturday 9am–8pm, and Sunday 7am–7pm; ask ABC for a written hour change under Exhibit A §4.3 at $25.95. Pick a staffing scenario: 1 Current $243k · 2 Keep Jennifer + ABC $151k (−37.6%), including 120 hrs ABC PTO backfill · 3 ABC only $132k (−45.5%; ~$19k cheaper than #2, if AGM role is not retained). Pair with an after-hours package plan.
