High volatility in repairs & maintenance expenses across years

Needs review

Repairs & maintenance category jumped from $167,950 in 2021 to $555,282 in 2023 (+231%), then fell to $99,703 in 2026 (-82%). This pattern suggests either deferred maintenance cycles or inconsistent expense classification.

Inconsistent boiler-related account usage and budgeting

Needs review

Multiple boiler accounts (1-60000, 3-60000, 3-65000, 3-85600) show erratic actuals vs. budgets, including months with zero actuals despite budgeted amounts (e.g., $833.33 monthly boiler repairs budget in 2021-2022), suggesting classification or reporting issues.

August 2023 financial statement is absent

Needs review

The statements set covers January 2021 through June 2026 (65 documents), but August 2023 is missing. This gap requires verification before relying on 2023 monthly trends or concluding that the year's reported totals are complete.

Expenses ran well above budget for three consecutive full years

Needs review

Reported expenses exceeded budget by $373,897.52 in 2022 ($2,007,354.60 actual vs $1,633,457.08 budget), $473,632.11 in 2023 ($2,044,977.95 vs $1,571,345.84), and $587,266.04 in 2024 ($2,072,295.37 vs $1,485,029.33). Those years also reported operating deficits of $180,897.46, $239,589.80, and $131,550.53, respectively; the board or management should explain drivers and budget-setting changes.

January 2024 boiler-replacement charge substantially exceeded stated budget
