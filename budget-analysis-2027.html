Budget treatment

Do not assume that reducing front-desk coverage or vendor work creates savings if the same work reappears as emergency repairs, overtime, resident-service failures, or unplanned capital spending.

Separate each maintenance item into:

  • routine operating maintenance;
  • repair or replacement with a quote;
  • reserve-study capital work; and
  • owner-responsibility work subject to policy review.
The current records do not contain enough quoted scope to put a reliable dollar amount on the heating-unit and pump work. It should remain an explicit 2027 risk allowance or project decision, not disappear inside a generic maintenance budget.

Evidence and method

  • Financial statements: extracted line entries in ../condo/data/condo.db, including gas account history for 2021 through July 2026 and 2026 reserve contribution account 5120. The historical projection sums entries whose statement section is Expenses, then separates the gas account.
  • Projection calculation: 2024 and 2025 are complete years. 2026 uses January-July actuals and estimates August-December excluding-gas expenses from the average of the same five months in 2024 and 2025. The 2027 trend rate is the midpoint of the observed 2024-2025 and projected 2025-2026 excluding-gas changes.
  • Existing financial review: ../condo/docs/financial_report.html, which identifies the 2026 gas annualization as a scenario, notes incomplete budget coverage, and records the multi-year reserve contribution and transfer patterns.
  • Reserve study: Falcon February 2025 Capital Reserve Replacement Fund Analysis, residential C-1, commercial C-25, and common-area C-51 summary tables, plus the funding projections. The source PDF remains in the local document library.
  • Local task database: the records titled “Execute 2026 reserve contribution catch-up and additional transfers,” “Review monthly board financial packages and resolve discrepancies,” and “Determine actions for Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac lending compliance.” These provide operational context and are treated as task-record evidence, not as independently audited balances.
  • Gas forecast method: average of full-year gas actuals for 2021-2023, which are the most complete pre-anomaly series in the extracted data, plus a stated 15% uncertainty buffer. The calculation is intentionally explicit so it can be replaced after post-repair billing history is available.
